## Governance authority

The STRATA Protocol governs its own development. Authority over the framework flows through the five strata documented at [strataprotocol.org/documentation/the-strata](https://strataprotocol.org/documentation/the-strata):

1. **Classification** — what kind of work this is, and how deep the derivation behind it must go
2. **Derivation** — vision, business requirements, product requirements, and engineering standards
3. **Authority Chain** — the operative constitution that bounds day-to-day work, issued as one edition per AI agent
4. **Execution Loop** — the human-gated session record of each Feature Release
5. **Artifact Trail** — the durable record of every decision

Decisions about the framework's direction, scope, and releases rest with the creator. Community input is welcomed through [issues](https://github.com/FlorantePascual/strata-protocol-docs/issues).
