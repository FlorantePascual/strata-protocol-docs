These are the real governance documents that produced StrataProtocol.org — public in the [strata-protocol-docs repository](https://github.com/FlorantePascual/strata-protocol-docs){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} under `docs/strata/`. Every link opens in a new tab. A stratum with a single document links straight to the file; a stratum with several links to its folder. Some Stratum 2 slots and the later execution-loop sessions are held back by design.

This platform was built under STRATA Protocol v1.0, which numbered requirements `REQ-NNN`, and its records keep those identifiers; FR-001 is its first [Feature Release](/documentation/reference#feature-release) under STRATA Protocol v1.2.

## Stratum 1 — Classification

- [Classification Record](https://github.com/FlorantePascual/strata-protocol-docs/blob/main/docs/strata/1.classification/classification-record.md){target="_blank" rel="noopener"} — the signed record fixing this project as Class 1, Category 1.3 — Platformization, before any specification was written.
